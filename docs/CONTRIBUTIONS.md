# RAIDER SAI — Project contribution statement

## Project ownership

I, Pavan Kumar Karsinapu, originated the RAIDER SAI electric-motorcycle project and carried the primary engineering work from the initial concept through the published project documentation and subsequent development.

My contribution covered the following workstreams:

1. **Concept formulation and research** — identified the retrofit concept, researched the electric traction architecture and evaluated the motor, battery, controller and transmission configuration.
2. **System architecture and design** — developed the mechanical system design and the integration approach for the 48 V BLDC motor, battery bank, controller, chain drive and four-speed motorcycle gearbox.
3. **Theory and engineering calculations** — prepared the project's analytical formulation and calculations for motor power and torque, nominal battery energy, drivetrain behaviour and vehicle-performance estimates. See MEASUREMENT_REGISTER.md for the current evidence classification and unresolved calculation limitations.
4. **Drivetrain development** — investigated the first two chain-drive arrangements, diagnosed the speed-transfer and gear-change problems, and developed the final documented custom-sprocket configuration.
5. **Testing and optimisation** — planned and led the project-level evaluation and iterative improvement process. The thesis preserves test procedures and selected reported outcomes, but not a complete calibrated raw-data package. Do not interpret every procedure as a completed, instrument-validated test.
6. **Technical documentation and publication** — prepared the technical report and project documentation and developed the published IRJET paper associated with RAIDER SAI.
7. **Subsequent development** — continued work on the project after the original workshop build, including the later supervisory-interface and embedded-control concept. The checked-in web application is a simulation-only UI demonstration, not a hardware controller.

## Workshop participation

The remaining six members of the seven-person student team participated in workshop fabrication and assembly under my direction. Their contribution is acknowledged in the published paper. This repository recognises their workshop support while distinguishing it from the project's primary concept development, research, design, analytical calculations, testing/optimisation leadership and documentation, which I carried out.
